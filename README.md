# Dubai Eats Atlas

A bilingual (Arabic/English) guide to Dubai restaurants: Google ratings, opening hours, what to order, cost for two, how to get there, and links to directions and menus.

Live site: https://iamaffanalikhan.github.io/dubai-eats/

Restaurant owners can request a listing from the "List your restaurant" section, by WhatsApp (+971 52 756 7142) or email (iamaffanalikhan2017@gmail.com).

Ratings and details were collected from Google Maps on 28 October 2026 and may have changed since.
